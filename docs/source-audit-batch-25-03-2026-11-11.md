## Editorial corrections and cautions

- **p65 corrected** to ask who spoke the ending prayer, rather than saying that the prophet was commanded to recite it. Quran 21:112 in Hafs reads «قال رب احكم بالحق», while other recitations read «قل رب احكم بالحق»; the new explanation states that distinction.
- **g36:** Bukhari 1091–1092 expressly mentions Maghrib as three units even during a journey; avoid citing Bukhari 1090 for this fact.
- **g37:** Bukhari 1012 describes the Prophet's prayer for rain and the two units of istisqa.
- **g39:** the reference to Al-Jinn 72:1 is an *example* of a chapter starting with «قل», **not** independent evidence that the total is five; the five chapter openings must be counted across Al-Jinn, Al-Kafirun, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas.
- **x34:** Quran 19:7 proves Yahya is named in Maryam; separately verifying his name is absent from Al-Kahf requires a whole-surah check.
