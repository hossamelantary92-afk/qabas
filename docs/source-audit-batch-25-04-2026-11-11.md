- p66: Quran 20:42 explicitly addresses Musa and his brother; [Quran.com tafsir](https://quran.com/ar/20%3A42/tafsirs/ar-tafseer-al-saddi) independently retrieved during this batch.
- q58: Quran 29:26 attributes the emigration statement to Lut. Retained attribution as recorded; requires individual direct primary-text fetch to mark externally verified in a rigorous per-ID roster.
- x38: the 29th juz starts at Surat al-Mulk, based on mushaf juz boundaries; verse 67:1 alone does not establish the juz number.
- x40: Quran 53:49 names al-Shi'ra; establishing the absence of the name Suhayl requires checking the complete surah rather than relying on 53:49 alone. Explanation clarified.
- g41: existing PDF study reference retained. A more directly citable hadith or juristic source can strengthen its evidence; no new URL added to this item.
- g42: mushaf ordering gives al-Kahf (18), Maryam (19), Ta-Ha (20); verse 19:1 does not by itself prove canonical surah placement.
- q59: question is “who was told ...”, not “who said ...”; the answer al-Samiri matches that formulation.
