## Focus notes
- **h34/h41**: Bukhari 6018 contains both honoring a guest and not harming a neighbor.
- **h35**: Bukhari 38 supports the reward for fasting Ramadan with faith and hope of reward.
- **h39**: Bukhari 5353 directly links caring for widows or poor persons to the merit of striving in God's cause.
- **h42**: Muslim 245 directly describes sins departing from the body upon proper ablution.
- **h44**: Muslim 1631 enumerates the three continuing forms of reward after death.
- **h52**: Muslim 2564a contains the precise sequence «لا تحاسدوا ولا تناجشوا ولا تباغضوا».
- **x60**: 81:1 names the opening of At-Takwir; the false opening is from 82:1 (Al-Infitar), not the same surah.
- **v64**: the correct continuation is «وأبيه» in 80:35.
