## Source quality and caution
- [Saudipedia Grand Mosque](https://saudipedia.com/en/grand-mosque), [Quba Mosque](https://saudipedia.com/en/quba-mosque), [Uhud](https://saudipedia.com/en/mount-uhud), [Safa-Marwa](https://saudipedia.com/en/safa-and-marwa) and [Hajj](https://saudipedia.com/en/hajj) are specific geographic or pilgrimage descriptions.
- [UNESCO Historic Cairo](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/89) and [Cordoba](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/313) identify the relevant mosques; [Egypt Ministry of Antiquities](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/ar/monuments/muhammad-ali-mosque) directly places the Muhammad Ali Mosque at Cairo Citadel.
- [Dar al-Ifta](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/11544/الاعتكاف-ومكانه-ومدته) directly explains itikaf and [Dar al-Ifta's fasting ruling](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/16832/بيان-ما-يلزم-المسلم-إذا-كان-مريضا-لا-يستطيع-الصيام-وفقيرا-لا-يستطيع-إخراج-الفدية) distinguishes permanent incapacity, fidya and temporary illness.
- `g03`: knowing Baqarah has 286 verses is not by itself exhaustive proof that it is longest in all surahs. Retained contextual.
- `x11`: need explicit length comparison Baqarah/Falaq from primary corpus. Retained contextual.
- `l24`: generic Jerusalem world heritage page cannot prove exact location of al-Aqsa within the old city. Retained contextual.
